MTBF, MTTR and availability: calculate and improve

Level: Intermediate Maintenance method · by SAHALI, MaintenanceIQ

Three indicators are enough to steer a machine’s reliability: mean time between failures (MTBF), mean time to repair (MTTR) and the resulting availability.

The formulas

  • MTBF = operating time ÷ number of failures.
  • MTTR = total repair downtime ÷ number of repairs.
  • Availability = MTBF ÷ (MTBF + MTTR).

Example

Illustrative example: a pump ran 2,000 hours over six months with 4 failures totalling 40 hours of repair. MTBF = 2,000 ÷ 4 = 500 h. MTTR = 40 ÷ 4 = 10 h. Availability = 500 ÷ (500 + 10) ≈ 98%.

Improve MTBF

Fix root causes (lubrication, alignment, looseness, contamination, heat, electrical issues) and monitor condition to act before failure.

Reduce MTTR

Critical spares in stock (especially far from suppliers), procedures and tools ready, fast diagnosis, machine history at hand.

Frequently asked questions

Is MTBF a guaranteed lifetime?

No. It is an average calculated from history: use it to follow a trend and compare, not to predict a specific failure.

What availability should I aim for?

It depends on the cost of downtime. Above all, compare your availability with itself over time.
