Air conditioner not cooling: checks before calling a technician

Level: Simple Buildings and installations · Air conditioning · by SAHALI, MaintenanceIQ

An air conditioner that blows without cooling very often has a simple cause: dirty filters or an outdoor unit that can no longer reject heat. A few checks without tools solve many cases. Low refrigerant, however, always needs a certified technician.

Warning signs

  • Air is barely cool.
  • The outdoor compressor runs non-stop.
  • The room no longer reaches the set point.
  • Ice on the pipes or the indoor unit.
  • Safety shutdown or error code.

Likely causes and how to check

Likely causeHow to check
Dirty indoor unit filtersOpen the cover, unit off, and look at the filters
Outdoor unit dirty, blocked or in full sun without airflowFins, leaves, objects in front, clearance around
Wrong mode or set pointRemote: cooling mode, set point, fan speed
Outdoor fan not runningWatch the fan while the compressor runs
Refrigerant shortage (leak)Diagnosis by a certified technician only

What to do

  1. Check cooling mode and set point on the remote.
  2. With the unit switched off: clean the filters as per the manual and let them dry completely.
  3. Clear the outdoor unit: nothing in front or on top, clean fins, free airflow.
  4. If ice forms: switch off and let it thaw before restarting.
  5. If nothing changes, or the outdoor fan does not turn: call a certified technician. Recharging without fixing the leak solves nothing.
Safety first. Switch off the power before opening the unit. The refrigerant circuit may only be handled by a certified professional. Your site’s rules and the manufacturer’s instructions always come first.

Frequently asked questions

How often should AC filters be cleaned?

According to the manual and use: in hot climates where the unit runs a lot, a check every few weeks is often needed.

Should the gas be topped up every year?

No. A refrigeration circuit is sealed: if refrigerant is missing, there is a leak to find and repair.
