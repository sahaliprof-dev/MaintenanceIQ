About MaintenanceIQ

MaintenanceIQ is a brand of SAHALI, sole trader (trade name Atelier Calibre) based in France. It helps small industrial sites and buildings that have neither sensors nor maintenance software to spot failures early and act at the right time.

Who is behind MaintenanceIQ

SAHALI learned maintenance where failures cost money: on production lines. He worked as a machine operator on packaging lines in the food industry, then as an operator and fitter in the heavy-vehicle industry, before assembling refrigerated truck chassis.

  • Industrial maintenance technician training (2024–2025).
  • Electrical clearance (French BR level).
  • First-level setting, operation and maintenance of industrial machines.
  • More than six years in industrial production.

The method

Simple, public rules: start from what the operator sees, hears and feels, measure consistently, compare with the machine’s own baseline, and decide with a traffic-light code. Vibration readings are read against the ISO 10816 zones.

What MaintenanceIQ does not do

It does not see your machine, does not predict a failure date, does not connect to your equipment and does not replace an on-site technician or mandatory inspections.
