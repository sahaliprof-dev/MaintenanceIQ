The formula

Breakdown cost = lost production (downtime hours × margin lost per hour) + labour (waiting, repair, overtime) + parts (emergency prices, express transport) + knock-on costs (lost product, late penalties, lost customers, backup equipment rental).

Why remote sites pay more

On islands, in remote regions or far from suppliers, the missing part often arrives by air or sea. Every day of waiting adds downtime. The same defect spotted a few weeks earlier (noise, heat, vibration) lets you order the part normally and replace it during a planned stop.
